Founder & Principal Advisor

Allison Ehrler-Meyer

Allison Ehrler-Meyer brings over two decades of leadership experience guiding individuals, teams, and leaders in mid-sized and Fortune 500 enterprises through complex organizational transformations, career transitions, and professional growth.

Her career spans coaching, organization effectiveness and employee engagement consulting, and large-scale organizational change across healthcare, financial services, energy, manufacturing, and government sectors.

Prior to founding Beacon Advantage Solutions, Allison held senior leadership roles in operations, organization development and effectiveness, and talent strategy at multiple Fortune 100 companies, where she developed and led coaching programs, employee development, and enterprise-wide transformation initiatives affecting employees across organizations.

This hands-on experience in complex environments guides her approach to helping individuals and teams find their unique strengths and signature paths.
